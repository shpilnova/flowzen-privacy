Privacy Policy for Flowzen

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Flowzen is a personal expense tracker that reads bank SMS notifications you already receive and turns them into categorized transactions — entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what data the app touches, where it goes, and what it never does.

  • All your transaction data is stored locally on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. We do not operate any server, and the app does not create an account for you.
  • The app never reads your Messages directly. iOS does not allow any third-party app to do that. Instead, you configure a Shortcuts Personal Automation yourself; when a matching message arrives, iOS hands its text to this app through Apple's official App Intents framework — the same mechanism any Shortcuts action uses.
  • The app makes zero network requests. All parsing happens on-device. The only data that ever leaves your device is handled by Apple for the optional Flowzen Pro subscription, described below.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking of any kind.

What data the app processes

  • Transaction data: amount, currency, merchant name, category, date, and the original SMS/CSV text it was parsed from. Stored only in the app's local database on your device.
  • Learned merchant categories: if you correct a transaction's category, the app remembers that merchant → category mapping locally, to categorize future transactions from the same merchant automatically.
  • CSV files you choose to import: read directly from the file you pick, processed on-device, never uploaded anywhere.

None of the above ever leaves your device.

Optional: Flowzen Pro (subscription)

Flowzen Pro is an optional, auto-renewable subscription that unlocks unlimited transaction history, Reports, Budgets, CSV import, and Home Screen widgets. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit:

  • We never see, collect, or store your payment details — Apple processes the transaction directly, and no card or billing information ever reaches us.
  • Your subscription status is checked locally on your device using Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not run our own server to track your subscription.
  • Apple may share limited purchase information (such as which product you purchased and its status) with us in aggregate form, governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can review, manage, or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Settings app → your Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from within Flowzen's Settings tab.

What the app does not do

  • No account creation, no login, no user profiles.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No sharing or selling of any data to third parties, other than the Flowzen Pro purchase flow handled by Apple as described above.
  • No access to your Messages app, contacts, photos, location, or any other data beyond what you explicitly provide (SMS text via Shortcuts, or a CSV file you choose to import).

Your controls

Because everything lives on your device, you are always in full control:

  • Settings → Delete all data permanently removes every stored transaction from the device.
  • Settings → Forget learned merchant categories clears the merchant → category memory.
  • Settings → Manage Subscription / Restore Purchases lets you review or cancel Flowzen Pro at any time.
  • Deleting the app itself removes its entire local database.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children. It has no account system and no data leaves the device except through the Flowzen Pro purchase flow handled by Apple, described above.
